# Kanto TMs that repeat Hoenn (S-ITEMS)

Owner rule (2026-11-14): no TM or item the player already got in Hoenn should show up again in Kanto (Battle Frontier
items and things like mints, Bottle Caps, good healing items, Full Heals and Rare Candies may repeat). TMs never break, so a
second copy is useless.

**Finding:** every one of the 50 TMs and all 8 HMs can already be had in Hoenn in this game. So **every TM spot in Kanto is a
repeat** — 59 spots in all (31 given by people, Gym Leaders, shops and the Game Corner, 28 Poké Balls on the ground).
Nothing has been changed yet; the owner picks what goes in their place.

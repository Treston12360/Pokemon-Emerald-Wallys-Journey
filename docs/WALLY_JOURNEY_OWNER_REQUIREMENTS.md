# Wally's Journey — Owner Requirements / Permanent Project Memory

**Authority:** This is the standing record of the owner's requested design for Pokémon Emerald: Wally's Journey. Read this file **before** making changes, planning work, or claiming completion. Preserve these requirements between sessions. When the owner changes a decision, update this file in the same commit. Do not silently change the design.

**Project:** A playable **GBA Pokémon Emerald ROM hack**, not a Python or PC-only game. Python is allowed for offline tooling/checks, not as a replacement for the ROM. Develop on `wally-journey-development`; verify before integrating/releasing. Build and emulator playtesting are required before calling a feature complete.

## Story, characters and world

- Emerald's core Hoenn adventure remains recognizable, but the story is rewritten/enhanced around **Wally's Journey**.
- **Norman remains the player character's father.** This is an explicit owner correction and must not be lost.
- Wally has **more rival battles** throughout the journey. His personality arc begins shy and unsure, then gradually becomes noticeably more confident, capable, and cool as the story progresses. Wally ultimately becomes **Hoenn Champion instead of Wallace**. Wallace is referred to as the **previous Hoenn Champion**.
- Wally is a prominent rival with an **extremely challenging, competitively cohesive team of six Hoenn Pokémon**. Optimize synergy, coverage, abilities, moves, held items, AI, and difficulty. Avoid copying another important character's signature ace. The precise final roster is **not confirmed here**; do not invent or freeze one without consulting the owner/record.
- May remains important as a rival/character.
- **Mega Evolution is restricted to Gym Leaders/gym trainers, the Elite Four, Champions, rivals, Steven Stone, and Battle Frontier opponents.** Ordinary route/world trainers must **not** Mega Evolve. Determine eligible gym-trainer battles deliberately; do not extend Mega Evolution to ordinary trainers.
- **Steven Stone gives the player the Mega Bracelet inside Wally's house after the player defeats Norman.** Steven acknowledges how much the player has grown, explains he found the bracelet while traveling in another region, and says he believes it can help the player reach new heights. This is the authoritative scene and timing; supersedes all earlier May/Steven and fifth-gym speculation.
- The relevant starter's Mega Stone should be available. Exact availability and Mega battle distribution still need implementation validation; do not claim they already work in the ROM.
- Gym Leader rematches should upgrade teams and grant/use a Mega Evolution where a leader did not have one previously.

## Quality-of-life mechanics

- Both **Mach Bike and Acro Bike** can be owned and used without returning to Rydel's shop to exchange them.
- **TMs are reusable/infinite**, not consumed when taught.
- **Field HM effects** still require the relevant Gym Badge/permission, but the player **does not have to teach the HM move to a party Pokémon** to use the field action.
- **HM acquisition/story dialogue overhaul:** Existing moments where other characters simply hand out HMs should use fitting normal dialogue instead where necessary; Gym Leaders will explain that unlocking an HM's field use through the badge means the player need not teach the move to any Pokémon, and the Gym Leader also awards the corresponding HM. Audit each HM, badge, map event and story dependency so the game never loses a required HM or locks progression.
- Pokémon can **relearn eligible moves conveniently**, in the spirit of Pokémon Legends: Arceus, without permanent lockout of previously learned moves. Final UI/implementation details need verification.
- An optional **game speed-up** should not pitch-shift, accelerate, or distort music; design for actual GBA emulator/hardware constraints and test rather than promising arbitrary speedups.
- **Following Pokémon:** party Pokémon can visibly follow the player in the overworld, with appropriate sprites/behavior and **correct shiny variants** for shiny Pokémon.
- **Shiny odds: 1 in 4,096** for ordinary shiny generation; audit applicable encounter, gift, breeding, and static-generation paths and document deliberate exceptions.
- **Champion title defenses:** after becoming Champion, the player should be able to defend the championship in repeatable challenges; design proper triggers, teams, and progression.

## Gen 1–9 ecology and complete Pokédex obtainability — HIGH PRIORITY

- **Every non-legendary/non-mythical Pokémon species from Generations 1–9 must ultimately be obtainable in a single-player save** through a combination of wild encounters, evolution, breeding, gifts, fossils, and appropriately implemented events/other methods. Do **not** require one ambient wild slot for every evolved species.
- Across all games, review **all available Pokédex habitat/ecology descriptions** and historical wild encounter locations, including older and newer mainline entries. Make Hoenn placements believable for climate, terrain, season/weather, tide, elevation, caves and water environment. Favor canonical Hoenn placements where appropriate. **Do not infer ecology from typing alone.**
- Preserve Hoenn's native wildlife and progression; don't indiscriminately dump every species onto routes.
- Make **starters, pseudo-legendary families, and Pokémon historically very hard to find genuinely extremely rare**: typically at most **1% conditional probability per encounter method/rod** where applicable, only in a very small number of appropriate locations. Repeated slots must be added to obtain effective probability. Check scarcity across the *whole region*, not just each map. Stronger evolved pseudo-legendaries must remain highly exceptional, if encountered wild at all.
- Treat elusive species such as Feebas, Spiritomb, Rotom and fossils through appropriate limited habitats, conditions or acquisition mechanics. **Legendary and mythical species must not appear as ordinary ambient grass/surf/cave encounters**; special encounters/events require their own explicit plan.
- A Pokémon's **wild level must never be below the level at which it evolves under this project's actual evolution rules**. Include custom Emerald Legacy-style level changes. For stone/friendship/other special evolution mechanics, manually check story progression rather than inventing a single level threshold.
- **Crucial owner clarification:** If a catchable pre-evolution can legitimately evolve into its later stage, the later stage **does not need any wild placement** if no believable location exists. All species still must be obtainable, with their actual evolution, breeding, item, form and event requirements validated. Prioritize *family obtainability + plausible ecology* over inflated counts of unique wild species.
- Preserve **Pokémon Emerald Legacy-inspired solo evolution levels** instead of relying on a **Linking Cord**. Explicitly rejected: stocking Linking Cords or requiring them for solo trade evolutions. The broader evolution design must be checked species-by-species; do not automatically force all item evolutions to become level evolutions. Known verified code examples include Machoke→Machamp 38, Graveler→Golem 38, Kadabra→Alakazam 42, Haunter→Gengar 42. Gen 5 Karrablast/Shelmet were temporarily configured to evolve at level 38; treat that as this project's provisional extrapolation, **not a verified Emerald Legacy canonical rule**.
- Single-player completion cannot depend on link trades, inaccessible version exclusives, mutually exclusive one-off evolution items, or unavailable evolution conditions. Verify item shop/pickup availability, repeatable acquisition when needed, breeding and form mechanics.

## Sub-1% encounters — confirmed owner clarification (2026-10-16)

- Some exceptionally elusive non-legendary Pokémon should have **effective wild encounter probabilities below 1%** when their established scarcity, historical games, and habitat ecology justify it. **1% is a ceiling for many rare species, not a universal floor or fixed target.**

- Existing ordinary encounter tables have 1% as their smallest slot. A claimed 0.1%, 0.5%, etc. requires a **real implemented/tested extra encounter-selection mechanic or other mathematically correct method**; do not fake sub-1% rates in the JSON table.
- Test effective per-method and per-location rates, and confirm rarity remains fair: extremely rare Pokémon must still be legitimately obtainable, without misleading UI odds or inaccessible conditions.
- **Status:** Confirmed design requirement; sub-1% runtime encounter implementation and empirical verification have **not** been certified.

## Wally's late-game personality — definitive owner direction (2026-10-16)

- **Wally must grow into someone unmistakably confident, charismatic, stylish, formidable, and genuinely cool.** He can remain kind, empathetic, and respectful, but **do not** portray late-game/Champion Wally as perpetually timid, hesitant, fragile, apologetic, or merely a sweet underdog. He should feel like an accomplished rival and imposing Champion who *knows* he is exceptionally good.
- Show the transformation gradually through decisive actions, sharper and more self-assured dialogue, compelling entrances, visible leadership in crises, increasingly commanding battle presence, strategic team decisions, and a striking Champion reveal. His confidence is earned, not sudden or unsupported.
- Avoid turning him into a cruel bully, arrogant caricature, or an imitation of another rival. He can be relaxed, witty, competitive, composed under pressure, and occasionally playful while still caring about people and Pokémon.
- Preserve the beginning of his arc as shy and uncertain, and ensure interactions with the player, Norman, family, May/Brendan, Steven, and Wallace reflect his growth. The final Champion confrontation should make the player think **'Wally is cool as hell and genuinely dangerous to battle.'**
- This explicit personality target overrides any previous narrative suggestion that treats kindness or perpetual modesty as more important than visible confidence and coolness.
- **Status:** Confirmed creative requirement; specific dialogue, sprites, cutscenes, trainer scripting and implementation are not yet verified.

## Mature Hoenn narrative reconstruction — OWNER APPROVED (2026-10-16)

**Scope and fidelity:** The owner explicitly approved every story improvement in the October 16 discussion. This is a **cohesive expansion and repair of Pokémon Emerald**, not a grimdark reboot. Preserve recognizable routes, Gym progression, Emerald's dual Magma/Aqua crisis, the iconic Rayquaza resolution, Norman as the player's father, Juan in Sootopolis, Wallace's prior Champion status, and Wally ultimately becoming a charismatic, formidable Champion. Keep charm, hope, humor and exploration. Rewrite only where it strengthens logic, character, pacing or consequences. All points below are approved **design requirements**, not claims of implementation.

### 1. Professor Birch, the starter, and the research journey
- Retain Birch's urgent encounter with a wild Pokémon and the iconic starter selection. Add credible motivation for why Birch entrusts the player with a rare partner and Pokédex: Norman's known reputation and connection to Birch, the player's demonstrated judgment, and responsible follow-up support.
- Give the Pokédex assignment meaningful research stakes. Birch studies unusual habitat changes, migration and weather patterns; player observations gradually connect to Magma/Aqua activity and the legendary imbalance.
- Do not make Birch implausibly reckless or omniscient. The player earns further responsibility through demonstrated accomplishments.

### 2. The authorities, Team Magma and Team Aqua
- Establish both groups' legitimate public fronts or activities: habitat conservation, development advocacy, restoration work, community aid or research. Give sincere ordinary members credible motivations; distinguish rank-and-file beliefs from escalating leadership decisions.
- Explain why organized authorities and adult trainers do not immediately dismantle the groups: incomplete evidence, deceptive fronts, limited jurisdiction, strategically timed distractions, and concurrent public-safety duties. Do not portray law enforcement and Champions as inexplicably idle.
- Team Magma's argument about housing, farming, economic mobility and useful land must be **genuinely persuasive**. Link those concerns to Norman's family history and actual Hoenn residents. Team Aqua's environmental concerns must also be serious and credible. Neither group's catastrophic methods are justified by those concerns.
- Show ideological disagreement and dissent inside the organizations as harmful actions escalate.

### 3. Devon Corporation's credible complexity
- Devon provides genuine jobs, equipment, research and regional prosperity but can still face credible criticism for industrial expansion, energy use, ecological impacts and economic power.
- Do not turn Devon into a secretly evil corporation. Show understandable tradeoffs, public accountability and differing views among affected NPCs.
- Tie Devon's technology and business relationships naturally to Team Aqua/Magma's debates and to Steven's personal conflict.

### 4. Mt. Chimney meteorite plan
- Give Magma a credible in-world geological hypothesis for using the meteorite and volcano rather than a vague, absurd plot device.
- Place warnings from scientists/researchers that their model ignores nonlinear and unpredictable volcanic consequences. Maxie's failure is hubris, selective evidence and willingness to gamble with others' safety, not lack of intelligence.
- Preserve the meteorite conflict, Mt. Chimney/Jagged Pass geography, and progression flags; ensure prior clues foreshadow the attempted scheme.

### 5. Legendary myth, orbs and Sky Pillar
- Lay out consistent ancient history linking Groudon, Kyogre, Rayquaza, the orbs, Sootopolis, Mt. Pyre, Seafloor Cavern and Sky Pillar.
- Ancient records should be incomplete, contested or mistranslated in plausible ways, explaining why Maxie and Archie mistakenly believe artifacts can control forces they cannot.
- Foreshadow Rayquaza without undermining its dramatic appearance. Keep the Emerald resolution but establish why awakening Rayquaza is the informed best option rather than an effortless last-minute deus ex machina.
- Respect the owner's requirement that Kyogre/Groudon encounters feel enormous, more threatening and harder to catch; no ball-based difficulty compensation.

### 6. Slateport/Lilycove submarine theft and response
- Rework the submarine theft as a credible operation involving advance planning, distraction, compromised information/access, or an insider, with believable security and timing.
- Captain Stern, Devon personnel, local authorities, and Steven should actively investigate and respond afterward. Show consequences and efforts to recover the submarine.
- Retain Emerald's plot order, named locations, and path to Seafloor Cavern; avoid creating a transport, badge or event-flag softlock.

### 7. Steven's values and family tension
- Develop Steven as Devon president's son who appreciates the company's contribution but can challenge it on environmental responsibility and ethical decisions.
- Steven should independently examine Team Magma/Aqua claims, ancient artifacts and the emerging crisis rather than showing up only to dispense key items.
- Give him concrete jobs during the crisis, credible relationships with Wallace and the League, and meaningful dialogue connected to his father's business.
- Preserve the confirmed **post-Norman Steven scene in Wally's house** gifting the Mega Bracelet and starter-specific Mega Stone with a personal speech about the player's growth and Steven's travels.

### 8. May/Brendan's sustained arc and closure
- Extend May/Brendan's growth beyond the original Lilycove rival stopping point. Add meaningful interactions through the escalating crisis, including research, relief or logistics work.
- Give their motivations dimension: they might start as an experienced, confident trainer and gradually discover a long-term identity combining research and battling.
- Provide a satisfying late-game rival confrontation and a meaningful resolution. Keep them distinct from Wally; they should not steal his Champion arc.

### 9. Magma/Aqua accountability and redemption
- Do not resolve the teams' moral failures with a single apology or instant universal forgiveness.
- After the crisis, show concrete ecological repair, aid deliveries, research cooperation and personal accountability. Some former members renounce the groups, others remain conflicted, and some residents remain angry.
- Maxie and Archie must face the specific damage caused by their overconfidence, while remaining capable of growth. Preserve hopeful tone without erasing consequences.

### 10. Establish coherent Pokémon League rules
- Define the Hoenn League's role in accrediting Gym Leaders, maintaining badge eligibility, authorizing Elite Four attempts, recognizing Champions and administering title defenses.

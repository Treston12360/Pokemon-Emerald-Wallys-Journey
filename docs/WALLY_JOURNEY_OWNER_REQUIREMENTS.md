# Wally's Journey — Owner Requirements / Permanent Project Memory

**Authority:** This is the standing record of the owner's requested design for Pokémon Emerald: Wally's Journey. Read this file **before** making changes, planning work, or claiming completion. Preserve these requirements between sessions. When the owner changes a decision, update this file in the same commit. Do not silently change the design.

**Project:** A playable **GBA Pokémon Emerald ROM hack**, not a Python or PC-only game. Python is allowed for offline tooling/checks, not as a replacement for the ROM. Develop on `wally-journey-development`; verify before integrating/releasing. Build and emulator playtesting are required before calling a feature complete.

## Story, characters and world

- Emerald's core Hoenn adventure remains recognizable, but the story is rewritten/enhanced around **Wally's Journey**.
- **Norman remains the player character's father.** This is an explicit owner correction and must not be lost.
- Wally has **more rival battles** throughout the journey. His personality arc begins shy and unsure, then gradually becomes noticeably more confident, capable, and cool as the story progresses. Wally ultimately becomes **Hoenn Champion instead of Wallace**. Wallace is referred to as the **previous Hoenn Champion**.
- Wally is a prominent rival with an **extremely challenging, competitively cohesive team of six Hoenn Pokémon**. Optimize synergy, coverage, abilities, moves, held items, AI, and difficulty. Avoid copying another important character's signature ace. The precise final roster is **not confirmed here**; do not invent or freeze one without consulting the owner/record.
- May remains important as a rival/character.
- **Mega Evolution is restricted to Gym Leaders/gym trainers, the Elite Four, Champions, rivals, Steven Stone, and Battle Frontier opponents.** Ordinary route/world trainers must **not** Mega Evolve. Owner exception (2026-10-24): reformed Maxie and Archie as title-defense challengers may Mega Evolve (Camerupt, Sharpedo). Determine eligible gym-trainer battles deliberately; do not extend Mega Evolution to ordinary trainers.
- **Steven Stone gives the player the Mega Bracelet inside Wally's house after the player defeats Norman.** Steven acknowledges how much the player has grown, explains he found the bracelet while traveling in another region, and says he believes it can help the player reach new heights. This is the authoritative scene and timing; supersedes all earlier May/Steven and fifth-gym speculation.
- The relevant starter's Mega Stone should be available. Exact availability and Mega battle distribution still need implementation validation; do not claim they already work in the ROM.
- Gym Leader rematches should upgrade teams and grant/use a Mega Evolution where a leader did not have one previously.

## Quality-of-life mechanics

- Both **Mach Bike and Acro Bike** can be owned and used without returning to Rydel's shop to exchange them.
- **TMs are reusable/infinite**, not consumed when taught.
- **Field HM effects** require the relevant Gym Badge and HM item, but **never require any party Pokémon to know, be able to learn, or be compatible with the HM move**. Press A on a Cut tree, smashable rock, Strength boulder, etc., to activate the field move via the normal interaction; Surf/Dive/Waterfall must work at their map prompts too. Party-member selection is an internal visual effect only. Fly and Flash remain accessible via field actions without teaching the moves. Preserve this rule for all eight HMs.
- **HM acquisition/story dialogue overhaul:** Existing moments where other characters simply hand out HMs should use fitting normal dialogue instead where necessary; Gym Leaders will explain that unlocking an HM's field use through the badge means the player need not teach the move to any Pokémon, and the Gym Leader also awards the corresponding HM. Audit each HM, badge, map event and story dependency so the game never loses a required HM or locks progression.
- Pokémon can **relearn eligible moves conveniently**, in the spirit of Pokémon Legends: Arceus, without permanent lockout of previously learned moves. Final UI/implementation details need verification.
- An optional **game speed-up** should not pitch-shift, accelerate, or distort music; design for actual GBA emulator/hardware constraints and test rather than promising arbitrary speedups.
- **Following Pokémon:** party Pokémon can visibly follow the player in the overworld, with appropriate sprites/behavior and **correct shiny variants** for shiny Pokémon.
- **Shiny odds: 1 in 4,096** for ordinary shiny generation; audit applicable encounter, gift, breeding, and static-generation paths and document deliberate exceptions.
- **Champion title defenses:** after becoming Champion, the player should be able to defend the championship in repeatable challenges; design proper triggers, teams, and progression.

## Gen 1–9 ecology and complete Pokédex obtainability — HIGH PRIORITY

- **Every non-legendary/non-mythical Pokémon species from Generations 1–9 must ultimately be obtainable in a single-player save** through a combination of wild encounters, evolution, breeding, gifts, fossils, and appropriately implemented events/other methods. Do **not** require one ambient wild slot for every evolved species.
- Across all games, review **all available Pokédex habitat/ecology descriptions** and historical wild encounter locations, including older and newer mainline entries. Make Hoenn placements believable for climate, terrain, season/weather, tide, elevation, caves and water environment. Favor canonical Hoenn placements where appropriate. **Do not infer ecology from typing alone.**
- Preserve Hoenn's native wildlife and progression; don't indiscriminately dump every species onto routes.
- Make **starters, pseudo-legendary families, and Pokémon historically very hard to find genuinely extremely rare**: typically at most **1% conditional probability per encounter method/rod** where applicable, only in a very small number of appropriate locations. Repeated slots must be added to obtain effective probability. Check scarcity across the *whole region*, not just each map. Stronger evolved pseudo-legendaries must remain highly exceptional, if encountered wild at all.
- Treat elusive species such as Feebas, Spiritomb, Rotom and fossils through appropriate limited habitats, conditions or acquisition mechanics. **Legendary and mythical species must not appear as ordinary ambient grass/surf/cave encounters**; special encounters/events require their own explicit plan.
- A Pokémon's **wild level must never be below the level at which it evolves under this project's actual evolution rules**. Include custom Emerald Legacy-style level changes. For stone/friendship/other special evolution mechanics, manually check story progression rather than inventing a single level threshold.
- **Crucial owner clarification:** If a catchable pre-evolution can legitimately evolve into its later stage, the later stage **does not need any wild placement** if no believable location exists. All species still must be obtainable, with their actual evolution, breeding, item, form and event requirements validated. Prioritize *family obtainability + plausible ecology* over inflated counts of unique wild species.
- Preserve **Pokémon Emerald Legacy-inspired solo evolution levels** instead of relying on a **Linking Cord**. Explicitly rejected: stocking Linking Cords or requiring them for solo trade evolutions. The broader evolution design must be checked species-by-species; do not automatically force all item evolutions to become level evolutions. Known verified code examples include Machoke→Machamp 38, Graveler→Golem 38, Kadabra→Alakazam 42, Haunter→Gengar 42. Gen 5 Karrablast/Shelmet were temporarily configured to evolve at level 38; treat that as this project's provisional extrapolation, **not a verified Emerald Legacy canonical rule**.
- Single-player completion cannot depend on link trades, inaccessible version exclusives, mutually exclusive one-off evolution items, or unavailable evolution conditions. Verify item shop/pickup availability, repeatable acquisition when needed, breeding and form mechanics.

## Living Hoenn — seasons, weather, lights, routines and wildlife (owner-approved wishlist, 2026-10-17)

**Goal:** A living, beautiful Hoenn. This section records the owner's complete brainstormed wishes, **not implemented/verified features**. Preserve GBA performance, legibility, seamless story progression and existing region identities.

### Four real seasons, each lasting 48 hours IRL

- Use **48 real-world hours per season**: **spring → summer → autumn → winter → spring**, one full cycle every **8 real-world days**. This **replaces the earlier month-based seasonal schedule**. Count elapsed wall-clock time, even while the game is closed, not playtime. Use a stable clock anchor to avoid reset on reload/map change; handle invalid RTC, clock rollback and emulator compatibility without corrupting saves. Do not force a season-selection option that the owner has not requested.
- Seasons must show real **map artwork/palette/tileset changes**, not only colored daylight filters:
  - **Spring:** new green growth, blooming flowers, blossoming trees, soft refreshing colors, intermittent spring rain.
  - **Summer:** lush leafy trees, green grass, warm sunlight, brighter clear weather, occasional afternoon storms.
  - **Autumn:** visible trees with varied **red, orange, gold/yellow and brown leaves**, fallen-leaf touches, changing grass and bushes, cooler light, overcast/rainy days. Different trees can change differently rather than all using one palette.
  - **Winter:** actual falling snow and snow/frost on trees/ground **where appropriate to geography**, including elevated/cooler places; chilly daylight, winter rain in lower coastal and tropical places, not snow everywhere.
- **No Black/White-style season-only paths:** no snow pile ladders, changed collision, seasonal obstacles, blocked roads or mandatory items hidden behind the clock. Map layout, warp access, Cut obstacles, scripted story sequences and available services remain the same every season. Seasonal visuals are aesthetic, not a progression mechanic.
- Small unobtrusive **“Spring/Summer/Autumn/Winter has arrived in Hoenn”** notice the first time the player goes outside after a change; only once per change and never during important cutscenes.

### Realistic local weather that changes naturally

- Each route/city has its own weather determined by **biome, elevation, season, terrain, coastline and neighboring weather fronts**, instead of the same weather throughout Hoenn or pure unrelated map RNG. A route may just happen to be rainy on a particular day; weather can differ between routes and towns while being consistent at borders.
- **Gradual weather transitions:** clouds gather, skies darken, wind begins, drizzle grows into steady rain or a storm, occasional distant thunder, then clearing skies. Avoid abrupt weather popping when crossing maps or saving and loading. Use deterministic/stable state as appropriate; do not require actual weather-service/internet access.
- **Fortree/Route 119** humid and regularly rainy, mist, occasional thunderstorms; **Route 113/Mt. Chimney** retain volcanic ash and wind; **Route 111 desert** mostly hot/dry, occasional sandstorms; **Routes 114–115/Meteor Falls/highlands** cool mist/rain and plausible winter frost/snow; **Slateport/Lilycove** coastal breezes and passing showers; **Pacifidlog/open-ocean routes** tropical wind, sun, showers, sea mist. Other locations similarly tailored, with reasonable adjacent-route continuity.
- Preserve special weather for **Groudon/Kyogre/Rayquaza story events**, map-specific hazards and underwater maps; never override required weather or make critical NPCs, routes, species or events inaccessible. Weather-specific wild encounters may change *rates* but must not make Pokédex completion require months of waiting.
- **PokéNav local weather forecasts** for towns/routes, using the same underlying weather schedule rather than invented unrelated predictions.

### Atmospheric day/night and automatic town lights

- Distinct visually convincing **dawn, morning, daytime, dusk, evening and night**, smoothly transitioning and maintaining readable map/character colors, consistent interiors and follower sprites.
- **Streetlights automatically illuminate at night**; glowing **house windows, porch lamps, street/shop lights** switch on where those fixtures/windows exist. Homes have varied schedules, some go dark late; shops may shut their lights, while Pokémon Centers stay lit overnight. Do not illuminate every building identically.
- Lights may turn on **earlier during overcast storms**; where feasible, light pools and subtle wet pavement/puddle reflections enhance rainy nights. In snowy evenings, warm windows contrast with cold outdoor scenery. Keep reflections/lightning tasteful, readable and performant, never strobing.
- Preserve the original game's environmental tone, with appropriate city-to-city differences.

### Responsive people, Pokémon and ambience

- **NPC schedules/routines:** people travel between sensible existing spots during day, evening and night; go home, visit shops, shelter from rain, use umbrellas, seek shade on hot days, or visit beaches on pleasant days. Dialogue reacts to season, weather and time and respects milestones. **Never** make Gym Leaders, HM/story NPCs, critical services or quests unavailable due to NPC schedules; avoid blocking doorways/paths.
- **Visible wildlife:** small appropriate Pokémon in grass/trees/sky/coastal water, flying overhead, moving through underbrush or briefly surfacing, plus occasional realistic weather/season reactions; not swarms that overwhelm the GBA or battles.
- **Follower reactions:** a following Pokémon can respond to rain, snow, sunny beaches, hot springs, landmarks and weather, where feasible without disrupting gameplay.
- Seasonal/time/weather encounter variations must **preserve every required species/form as obtainable on a single save** without real-month waits; rare pseudo-legendaries stay appropriately rare. Preserve established lore/ecology placement, and Deerling/Sawsbuck seasonal forms should match the newly agreed **48-hour cycle**.
- **Ambient audio:** forest wind/leaves, rain against rooftops, insects/crickets at night, distant thunder, birds, water and waves; mix subtly under original music and do not alter music speed or pitch.
- Tasteful extras: falling/blowing leaves, seasonal blossoms, small environmental discoveries, optional weather-reactive NPC conversations/rewards, and **visible recovery after the legendary weather crisis** (repair work, cleaned-up environments, public reactions using appropriate existing locations).
- Locations should feel distinct across conditions: e.g., Fortree at autumn dusk with red/orange/gold trees, rain, warm windows and people seeking shelter.

# Belt stalls (Brennan 2026-11-20 20:15) - session 014PwRDj

**Report:** the 20b build stutters/freezes biking, walking in and out of towns and crossing map borders. His
WallysJourney_stalls.txt: `belt` 150-1680 ms in single frames (Route 111 0:26, Route 112 0:27), even with
`belt rebuilds 0`. Asked: no synchronous belt stalls; belt work under ~1.5 ms a frame; look at buildings.bin (53 MB,
dedupe); pre-warm neighbours; verify under 1-2 ms biking.

## Cause (measured, not guessed)
A sampling profiler (test aid `WJPC_PROFILE=1`, sdl2.c: samples the main thread every ms while belt pieces are built,
writes WallysJourney_profile.txt, named with llvm-addr2line) on Route 119's belt (100 pieces):

| where the time went (before) | share |
|---|---|
| NearestBeltMap (which loaded map is nearest a belt cell) | 39% |
| VoxelRelief_EdgeFill / EdgeFillAt | 15% |
| BorderIsPrimaryOnly | 10% |
| Voxel_IsFrlg (linear list) | 5% |
| VoxelWorld_GetInstanceAt, MemoGet, BeltOld, ... | rest |

The belt builder asks the same per-cell questions dozens of times per cell (every vertex, every neighbour, every
layer), and each answer walked every placed map. Plus ~80 test switches read with getenv() in per-cell code (on
Windows each walks the whole environment).

## Fix
- `voxel_env.h`: test switches read once (cached by name address) in the scenery code.
- `voxel_world.c` CellMemo: per-cell answers of NearestBeltMap, EdgeFillAt, BorderMetatile, BorderArtMap kept until
  the placed maps or belt tile sets change (`sCellGen`, `VoxelWorld_CellGen()`); BorderIsPrimaryOnly per map;
  Voxel_IsFrlg remembers its last answer. `voxel_mesh_builder.c`: BeltField and BorderWaterLevel kept per point the
  same way. `WJPC_NO_CELL_MEMO=1` turns all of it off (pictures compared: identical, Routes 119/121, Lavaridge, Mt Pyre summit).
- `voxel_gl.c`: piece building stops after a time budget (default 1 ms, `WJPC_BELT_BUDGET_MS`), at least one piece a
  frame, old belt shown meanwhile; maps coming into/out of reach rebuild the belt that way too, not all in one frame
  (`WJPC_BELT_FULL_ON_WORLD=1`: as before); belt radius 40 again except Mt Pyre outside/summit (64)
  (`WJPC_BELT_RADIUS=n`).

## 1. Fixed ordering without inventing calendar dates

The story is *one fixed history*. Its partial order is sufficient without inventing years:
1. **Historical Rocket raid:** Young Varnell and child Aria are separated; young Varnell is **forced outside**. A Rocket member still has Clefairy captive. A stable receiving window from a future ARIA opens. Aria pauses to hear her father, won't leave Clefairy. Future Varnell believes her recorded death is imminent and jumps **to save her**, despite Blue's warning. **Only at his entry** the past endpoint destabilizes; the ceiling starts falling, Clefairy breaks free and runs to Aria, and Aria/Clefairy/future Varnell perish. The unidentified male body is unrecognizable, mistaken by some residents as Rocket. Young Varnell survives outside and blames Rocket. **This event always happened**; there is no separate original version of the collapse.
2. **After the raid:** Young Varnell survives and lives the years of grief that lead to Project ARIA. Blue eventually loses to Red and Varnell offers him work, establishing their relationship. **OPEN:** exact calendar relation of the raid and Blue's encounter with Red, and ages; do not assert an exact year.
3. **Before player moves to Littleroot:** Varnell has captured Hoopa in Hoenn and begins opening mostly spatial rifts. The unusual Hoenn wild Pokémon predate the player's starting journey.
4. **Hoenn player becomes Champion:** Groudon/Kyogre/Rayquaza crisis and Hoenn progression occur independently of Varnell's earlier Hoopa operations. The player, **never Wally**, is then invited to investigate Kanto.
5. **Kanto chapters 1–12:** Real public stabilization work masks further legendary containment; experiment order and motivation detailed in source documents.
6. **Kanto chapter 13:** The same **future Varnell** at point 1 makes the fatal jump. In his experienced time-line, he has already heard the Tower's historical accounts and investigated the Clefairy echo, but he does **not** realize he causes the collapse. No paradox-resolving narrator speech; leave adult victim identity as a retrospective inference.

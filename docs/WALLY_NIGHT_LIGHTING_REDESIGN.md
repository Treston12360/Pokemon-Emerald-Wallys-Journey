# Native nighttime lighting polish — Wally's Journey

**Owner art direction (2026-10-17):** PC and MART **letters** should look like restrained neon. The sign backplates, border, door, wall and roof must **not** glow. Windows should glow *inside the window panes*, not via bright circles glued over the facade. The aim is attractive original-GBA pixel art, inspired by atmospheric games but built with pokeemerald-expansion-compatible rendering.

## First implementation: sign-letter prototype (this PR)

- Inspected the actual General tileset's metatiles: PC sign `0x062` uses tile `0x147`; Mart sign `0x042` uses tile `0x149`; both reference palette slot 1. **Do not mark all colors in slot 1 as light-blended:** that palette is widely shared by the rest of the General tileset.
- Generated two tiny **transparent 16x16, 4bpp OBJ images** retaining only the letter pixels from the corresponding sign tile (original color-index 13 core and 11 detail). Background and border stay untouched, and tiles are all zeros outside those glyph pixels.
- Center lettering is soft warm white/orange; Mart lettering is a cool near-white/blue. Different reserved, time-blend-immune OBJ palette tags keep the lettering visible at night without bleaching the rest of the building. Use **normal OBJ rendering**, not a blended 16x16 rectangle.
- Reuses existing sign location detection, time/weather schedule and automatic spawning. Existing Pokémon Center and Mart buildings remain unchanged.
- Removed automatically generated **32x32 window blob sprites** and their broad "nearest door" lookup. Until alternate palette-based window art exists, windows retain their ordinary night appearance. Script-authored lights, streetlamps and other owner-approved systems remain otherwise untouched.

## Next step: proper window-pane lighting

Follow the [pokeemerald-expansion day/night guide](https://rh-hideout.github.io/pokeemerald-expansion/tutorials/dns.html). Mark *isolated* window color indices with `.pla` or supply alternate nighttime `.pal` data plus `swapPalettes` **after inspecting each town tileset**. Most ordinary palettes are shared between windows, trees, fences and roofs; do not light the whole palette or unrelated pixels. Where necessary, reassign window metatiles to a dedicated palette with preserved daytime colors. Prototype in Rustboro first, then migrate other towns after visual approval.

## Emulator acceptance checklist

- [ ] At noon, sign lettering matches normal Emerald; no new sign blobs.
- [ ] At evening and night, **only the PC/Mart glyph pixels glow**, with complete transparency outside the letter mask. Check sign alignment and color in mGBA at native 240x160, not only with emulator smoothing.
- [ ] On different town tilesets, the lettering stays correctly positioned without making sign backgrounds or building edges luminous.
- [ ] House doors and windows show **no autogenerated circles**, even in rain.
- [ ] Compare clear night, dusk, heavy rain and story weather; verify color is stable and no ugly pulsing/flicker.
- [ ] Verify window palette prototype, later, before calling house lights finished.
- [ ] Confirm no sprites/palette leaks, character occlusion, broken map warps or noticeable FPS loss after entering/exiting towns repeatedly.
- [ ] Confirm success on the current Emerald CI job and actual GBA emulator.

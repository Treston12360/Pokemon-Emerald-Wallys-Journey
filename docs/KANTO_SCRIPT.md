## Chapter 0 — The call (Littleroot → Slateport)  · DRAFT v2 (longer)

**Trigger:** first time the player wakes at home after the first Champion win (Hall of Fame done).

**0.1 Mom (downstairs, by the TV)**
> MOM: Well, look who's up! Good morning, Champion.
> ...I don't think I'll ever get tired of saying that.
> The phone hasn't stopped ringing, you know. Reporters, neighbours, people I haven't heard from since we
> left Johto. Everybody wants to say they know you.
> Oh! One of those calls was Professor Birch.
> He asked you to come over to the lab as soon as you're up. He said it wasn't urgent...
> But he called twice. Birch doesn't call twice.
> Go on. I'll keep breakfast warm.

**0.2 Birch (lab)**
> BIRCH: {PLAYER}! Sorry to drag you over so early. Sit, sit.
> You remember the Pokémon that started turning up all over Hoenn? The ones that had no business being here?
> I've spent months on it. Pokémon from regions an ocean away, living in our grass like they were born there.
> No migration, no shipments, no explanation. They're just... here.
> Last night Professor Oak called me from Kanto.
> It's started there too. And it isn't slowing down, it's getting worse. Whole routes changing in weeks.
> Oak has a team looking into it. The best researcher in Kanto is leading it, apparently.
> And he asked for you. By name.
> He said that you've already stood in the middle of one crisis and walked out the other side, and he'd
> rather have that on his team than another theory.
> ......
> I won't pretend I'm thrilled. You only just got home.
> But I'd be lying if I said I didn't agree with him.
> (Birch hands over the S.S. Ticket.)
> {PLAYER} received the S.S. TICKET!
> BIRCH: The S.S. Anne leaves from Slateport. Oak will meet you at the dock in Vermilion City.
> I'll talk to your mother. ...Actually, she probably already knows. She always does.
> {PLAYER}... be careful, all right? Kanto isn't home.

**0.3 Mom (on the way out)**
> MOM: Kanto, hm?
> Your father left for somewhere far away once, too. I spent a long time waiting by the phone.
> ......
> But you're not him, and this isn't that. You're going because someone needs help.
> Call me when you get there. Every once in a while, okay? Even just to say you ate something.
> I'm proud of you. Now go, before I change my mind and hide your shoes.

**0.4 Wally (Slateport harbour, by the S.S. Anne)**
> WALLY: Leaving without saying goodbye? That's cold, even for you.
> Birch told me. Kanto, huh.
> ......
> You know, a year ago I couldn't walk up Route 111 without stopping to catch my breath.
> Now I'm standing here telling the Champion not to worry about the Champion's seat.
> Funny how that works.
> Don't worry about the title. I'll keep the seat warm. I'm good at that.
> ...Hey. One more thing.
> Kanto has its own League. Eight badges, same as here.
> Everyone over there grew up hearing Kanto is where the real trainers come from.
> If you're going all that way anyway... go take their badges. All eight.
> They'll take one look at a trainer from Hoenn and think you got lucky. That our League is the easy one.
> Don't let them. Prove them wrong. Show them what Hoenn is made of.
> I'll keep the seat warm for you. And come back. I still owe you a rematch.

**0.5 Boarding**
> SAILOR: Ticket, please. ...The Champion! It's an honour. Welcome aboard the S.S. Anne.
> (fade to black)

## Chapter 1 — The investigation team (Oak's lab)  · DRAFT v2

**1.1 Introductions**
> OAK: {PLAYER}, allow me to introduce Dr. Elias Varnell.
> If you've read anything about space-time research in the last twenty years, you've read his work.
> He is the finest researcher in Kanto. I'd say the world, but he'd only argue.
> VARNELL: You flatter me, Samuel. And you exaggerate.
> {PLAYER}. It's a real pleasure.
> I read every report from Hoenn. Groudon. Kyogre. Rayquaza, brought down from the sky.
> And in every report, the same name, right in the middle of it.
> Legendary Pokémon. Forces that shape the land and the sea and the sky.
> I've spent my whole life studying Pokémon like that. Their traces, their old records, the marks they leave
> in space and time. Always from a distance. From behind the glass of an instrument.
> You stood right in front of them. And you're still here.
> I'm leading the investigation into these anomalies. I'd be very glad to have you with us.
> OAK: And this is my grandson, Blue. He's been working with Dr. Varnell for... how long now?
> BLUE: ......
> BLUE: Long enough.
> BLUE: So this is the Champion you shipped in from Hoenn.
> Gramps, we had this handled. I've been mapping these things for months.
> OAK: Blue, nobody is saying you haven't—
> BLUE: Then why is there a stranger standing in the lab?
> ......
> BLUE: Whatever. Do what you want.
> Just stay out of my way, Champion.
> (Blue leaves.)
> OAK: ...Forgive him. He hasn't had an easy few years.
> VARNELL: He'll come around. Blue's a good young man. He just doesn't like sharing his work.
> I suppose I taught him that.

**1.2 The first assignment**
> VARNELL: Now. Down to business.
> We call them anomalies, but really they're tears. Small breaks in space, where something from somewhere
> else slips through.
> Something is happening inside Mt. Moon. Our instruments call it a "meteorite anomaly". The hikers call it
> a light in the tunnels that won't stay in one place.
> I'd like you to look into it.
> I built this for exactly this kind of work.
> {PLAYER} received the RIFT SCANNER!   (name approved by owner)
> VARNELL: If you find a tear, point the scanner at it. It will read it, and then seal it.
> Every tear we close tells us a little more about what's causing them.
> Oh, and one practical matter.
> Since the Rocket years, the League has locked down anywhere that could be dangerous. Only licensed Kanto
> trainers get in. The same goes for field moves: your Hoenn licenses don't count on this side of the sea.
> So I've taken the liberty of registering you for the Kanto Gym challenge.
> Consider it my thanks in advance.
> OAK: Pewter City is on the way to Mt. Moon. Brock's Gym is there.
> He'll give you a proper Kanto welcome, I'm sure.
> Good luck, {PLAYER}. And thank you for coming all this way.

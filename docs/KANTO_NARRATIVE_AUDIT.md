## The three distinct revelations (LOCKED)

1. **Chapter 3b:** Blue tells the player that Varnell had a daughter who died during a Team Rocket attack, but he does NOT know her name. Varnell introduces Project ARIA as a research machine. Chapter 4's ghostly Clefairy cries "Aria"; in Chapter 5 Blue reasonably assumes it refers to the project. Attentive players MAY guess the daughter is Aria early and falsely assume the project is a memorial.
2. **Chapter 12:** In the hidden Five Island facility, Blue and the player learn Aria was the daughter, the Clefairy was hers, and Project ARIA was secretly engineered to retrieve the living child from the past, with catastrophic risk to everyone else.
3. **Chapter 13:** After the legendary battle Varnell's precharged one-way retrieval gateway opens to Pokémon Tower during the original Rocket raid. **Varnell believes the historically recorded death of Aria is only moments away.** She hears him but will not leave without her captive Clefairy. With the window showing her final moments approaching, he feels forced to act: **if he cannot bring her out, he must go in himself to save her**. Blue warns the machine was built to bring someone OUT, not send someone IN. Varnell jumps IN anyway, **not knowing his jump creates the collapse he expects Team Rocket to cause**. **Only his reverse crossing begins the overload**; Clefairy escapes after the ceiling starts falling and runs to Aria, and all three die. This was **always** the cause in a **single closed timeline**, not a rewritten timeline. An unrecognizable adult male corpse from that historical collapse was future Varnell, but nobody knew. Varnell never realizes he caused his daughter's death; Blue/player can infer the truth without a full explanatory monologue. His fate is strongly implied to be death, even if it is not explicitly confirmed.

**Character truth:** Rocket DID raid the Tower, DID steal Clefairy, DID endanger Aria and DID steal Varnell's research; Varnell sincerely blamed them for her death. Rocket did **not directly cause** the falling ceiling. Do not have authorial narration falsely confirm that it did. Old residents may reasonably believe it.

## Consistent in-world physics (story design, not a claim about official canon)

- **Hoopa** opens passages; its unstable use mostly swaps creatures between *places* in the present; rare uncontrolled openings intersect different *times*.
- **Humans CAN cross** rifts. Rifts tend to form in wild habitats where Pokémon are much more likely to encounter them; spatial visitors can stay after a portal closes. Do not introduce an arbitrary rule that only Pokémon can travel.
- **Temporal travelers** are tethered to their original moment until the connection is anchored. Kiyoshi disappears because an uncontrolled temporary connection expires, **not because the player seals it**. The player genuinely tries to help. Varnell's concern may be real.
- **Dialga** would stabilize/choose WHEN, **Palkia** WHERE, **Giratina/Distortion World** supplies a needed dimensional intermediary. The **Griseous Orb** allows Giratina to be located/bound; all equipment should be prepared BEFORE Orb handover. Giratina does not simply revive a dead person. ARIA attempts to access Aria while she was alive.
- The finished ARIA gateway is engineered for **retrieval** from the past into the present. Reverse entry was never engineered/tested; Varnell's added mass/energy moving in the wrong direction overloads the destination anchor. Blue knows the general one-way restriction from legitimate public technology, not Varnell's hidden capture operation.
- A **stable window BEFORE the jump** is essential: Aria does not cross because she *refuses to abandon Clefairy*, not because humans are metaphysically unable. Varnell's jump triggers the first failure. Neither he nor Blue can see a ceiling collapsing before that jump.
- History is fixed: no branching timelines, no original version where only Rocket caused the collapse, no reset of records. The past contains *young Varnell alive* and an *older future Varnell's unidentified body* simultaneously.

## The three distinct revelations (LOCKED)

1. **Chapter 3b:** Blue tells the player that Varnell had a daughter who died during a Team Rocket attack, but he does NOT know her name. Varnell introduces Project ARIA as a research machine. Chapter 4's ghostly Clefairy cries "Aria"; in Chapter 5 Blue reasonably assumes it refers to the project. Attentive players MAY guess the daughter is Aria early and falsely assume the project is a memorial.
2. **Chapter 12:** In the hidden Five Island facility, Blue and the player learn Aria was the daughter, the Clefairy was hers, and Project ARIA was secretly engineered to retrieve the living child from the past, with catastrophic risk to everyone else.
3. **Chapter 13:** After the legendary battle Varnell's precharged one-way retrieval gateway opens to Pokémon Tower during the original Rocket raid. Aria hears him but will not leave without her Clefairy. Blue warns the machine was built to bring someone OUT, not send someone IN. Varnell jumps IN anyway. **Only his reverse crossing begins the overload**, causing the old ceiling collapse that killed Aria and Clefairy. This was **always** the cause in a **single closed timeline**, not a rewritten timeline. An unrecognizable adult male corpse from that historical collapse was future Varnell, but nobody knew. Varnell never realizes he caused his daughter's death; Blue/player can infer the truth without a full explanatory monologue. His fate is strongly implied to be death, even if it is not explicitly confirmed.

**Character truth:** Rocket DID raid the Tower, DID steal Clefairy, DID endanger Aria and DID steal Varnell's research; Varnell sincerely blamed them for her death. Rocket did **not directly cause** the falling ceiling. Do not have authorial narration falsely confirm that it did. Old residents may reasonably believe it.

## Consistent in-world physics (story design, not a claim about official canon)

- **Hoopa** opens passages; its unstable use mostly swaps creatures between *places* in the present; rare uncontrolled openings intersect different *times*.
- **Humans CAN cross** rifts. Rifts tend to form in wild habitats where Pokémon are much more likely to encounter them; spatial visitors can stay after a portal closes. Do not introduce an arbitrary rule that only Pokémon can travel.
- **Temporal travelers** are tethered to their original moment until the connection is anchored. Kiyoshi disappears because an uncontrolled temporary connection expires, **not because the player seals it**. The player genuinely tries to help. Varnell's concern may be real.
- **Dialga** would stabilize/choose WHEN, **Palkia** WHERE, **Giratina/Distortion World** supplies a needed dimensional intermediary. The **Griseous Orb** allows Giratina to be located/bound; all equipment should be prepared BEFORE Orb handover. Giratina does not simply revive a dead person. ARIA attempts to access Aria while she was alive.
- The finished ARIA gateway is engineered for **retrieval** from the past into the present. Reverse entry was never engineered/tested; Varnell's added mass/energy moving in the wrong direction overloads the destination anchor. Blue knows the general one-way restriction from legitimate public technology, not Varnell's hidden capture operation.
- A **stable window BEFORE the jump** is essential: Aria does not cross because she *refuses to abandon Clefairy*, not because humans are metaphysically unable. Varnell's jump triggers the first failure. Neither he nor Blue can see a ceiling collapsing before that jump.
- History is fixed: no branching timelines, no original version where only Rocket caused the collapse, no reset of records. The past contains *young Varnell alive* and an *older future Varnell's unidentified body* simultaneously.

## Plot-hole and consistency audit

**CRITICAL — requires deliberate revision, cannot be asserted fixed just because these notes exist:**

1. **Chapter 3 already IN GAME contradicts new Kiyoshi direction.** Current script: Varnell commands player to close rift; Kiyoshi returns screaming. New direction: Varnell asks to stabilize/save him, the tether expires independently. Rewrite only after owner approval of exact new dialogue; change data/scripts/aria_ch3.inc, rebuild/test Windows.
2. **Ch.4 lore & witness testimony:** The earlier script says a girl died during Rocket's attack; that's still true. It must NOT explicitly say Rocket detonated the ceiling. Rocket is proximate cause of dangerous raid, future portal is immediate cause of fall.
3. **League roster outdated in older chapter table / scripts:** Chapter 6 must say **Mimi, the Copycat (Normal Gym)** rather than Sabrina; E4 now **Koga -> Lorelei -> Bruno -> Sabrina -> Lance**, Agatha removed. Koga after-battle line "Champion awaits" no longer appropriate for room 1. Janine is Ghost, not Poison. S-LEAGUE2 remains unimplemented.
4. **Orb custody logic:** Why does a newly Kanto-certified Champion access the restricted vault, why does a guard accept a forged scientific transfer, and how can Lance notice only after Orb is delivered? Define a transfer ledger, limited clearance, and believable late audit before scripting. Player must not appear to knowingly steal it.
5. **Blue's knowledge:** Public one-way retrieval theory and danger must be established earlier, while Blue never suspects Varnell or knows Aria's name or the hidden legendaries until final reveal.
6. **Final machine autonomy:** Explain why loss of captured legendary Pokémon in battle does not immediately stop a precharged window; ensure defeat has visible effects while program runs on stored power.
7. **Body recognition:** Older Varnell's body and distinguishing possessions must be unrecoverably damaged so witnesses do not identify the famous scientist. Avoid invented forensic claims. Young Varnell is alive somewhere else in this same period.
8. **Kiyoshi physics:** Hoopa's uncontrolled temporal rift predates Dialga; why an uncontrolled pull decays while controlled machine holds a target must be consistent.
9. **Giratina / capture logistics:** Specific failed experiment must point to Distortion World, and Orb must make an already prepared Giratina capture feasible quickly. Explain how four legendary captives can be used in a final trainer fight and freed after.
10. **Clefairy's location:** Rocket steals Clefairy; Aria looks for it; the fall kills both. Map exactly where each is at the final moment to avoid an impossible chase or contradictory ghost lore.
11. **Quest/photo knowledge:** Blue can't have spent years walking past an obviously identifiable signed picture. Keep mementos private, photo on an optional later return visit; explain why player has the errand there.
12. **Physical timing:** Stable portal -> father's call -> daughter stops/refuses -> Blue warns -> father jumps -> *first* overload -> collapse -> window closes. No "Aria look out, the ceiling!" before the jump.

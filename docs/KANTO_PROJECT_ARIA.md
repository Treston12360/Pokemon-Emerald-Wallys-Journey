# Kanto: Project ARIA — design doc

Status: **design, owner-approved decisions recorded 2026-11-13.** Nothing here is built yet.
Read this before touching anything Kanto. Owner rules from `WALLY_JOURNEY_OWNER_REQUIREMENTS.md` still apply
(show pictures before visual/wording changes, no guessing, GBA saves must import).

Goal from the owner: **a far more narrative story than Hoenn had.** Every chapter should have a scene,
a reveal or a choice, not just a gym.

---

## 1. Locked decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Player | Not Wally. The player beat Champion Wally in Hoenn and is the Champion going in. |
| Era | After FRLG. Team Rocket is disbanded and **never appears**, not even as hired hands. |
| Villain | **Dr. Elias Varnell.** His daughter **Aria** was killed as a bystander in a Rocket attack. |
| The machine | **Project ARIA** — "Anomalous Rift Integration Array". Named after his daughter; the player only learns this at the reveal. |
| Legendaries | Hoopa (taken first), Dialga, Palkia, Giratina, held in containment machines. |
| Varnell's ending | **Lost to the rift.** Giratina drags him into the Distortion World. Tragic, not redeemed. |
| Wally | **Small cameos only** (calls / messages). Kanto is the player's story. |
| Blue | Former Champion, now the 8th leader (Viridian). Rejects the player, investigates on his own, respect earned slowly. No quippy banter. |
| Red | **Optional superboss only.** No story role. Final Champion-defense challenger. |
| Oak | Introduces Varnell. Trusts him; is shaken by the reveal. |
| Items | All Hoenn items, HMs and badges carry over. Nothing in Kanto is gated by an item. |
| Return | After Kanto the player returns to Hoenn to defend the title (see §6). |

## 2. Aria and Varnell (the emotional core)

- Aria died years ago, **caught in a Rocket attack** — wrong place, wrong time. Random and senseless,
  which is exactly why Varnell can't accept it. **Decided 2026-11-13: during the Silph Co. takeover,
  while visiting him at work.**
- **Rule for every clue: it only makes sense looking back.** Nothing may give the twist away early.
- At Saffron (ch. 6) Varnell goes quiet, won't enter Silph, and sends the player alone. It reads as a
  scientist protecting his career; after the reveal it's a father who can't walk into the place she died.
- Varnell is not a cartoon villain. He's kind, brilliant and genuinely helpful to the player for most
  of the game. He **knowingly** risks the world to get her back.
- ARIA is built to fold space and time open far enough to pull Aria back from the moment before she died.
  The "displacements" across Kanto are its test runs leaking.

### How the name hides in plain sight
"Project ARIA" is said all game and sounds like any acronym. Clues the player can miss:
- A child's drawing in his office signed "aria" in lowercase — a girl holding a **Clefairy** (pays off the Lavender ghost).
- Staff: "The Director won't let anyone else touch ARIA's settings."
- The holding bay terminal: "Good night, Aria."
- Mr. Fuji at Pokémon Tower mentions a grieving father who visits a grave "with no Pokémon name on it."

**Reveal:** the photo on Varnell's desk, labelled *Aria, age 7*. The player realises the machine is her.

## 5. Levels and EXP (simulated — see `tools/exp_curve_full.py`, `tools/exp_curve_kanto.py`)

- **Normal EXP. No Kanto EXP cut.** Gen 7 scaled EXP already slows over-levelled teams.
- A player who beats every Hoenn trainer reaches Champion Wally at about **55** (top ~58);
  with the post-game one-time fights, about **60**.
- **Route trainers:** fixed levels per area, about **5 below** where the simulation says the player is
  (the same gap Hoenn has at around level 20–29).
- **Wild Pokémon:** fixed, about **4 below** the local trainers. Not scaled.
- **Gyms, Elite Four, Varnell:** scaled like Hoenn's bosses (`wj_trainer_scaling.h`: minimum + margin over
  your highest, locked at first fight).

| Fight | Player avg (sim) | Boss ace minimum | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gyms 1–2 | 58–62 | 64 | +2 |
| Gyms 3–4 | 62–66 | 68 | +2 |
| Gyms 5–6 | 66–70 | 72 | +2 |
| Gyms 7–8 (Blue) | 70–73 | 76 | +3 |
| Kanto E4 | 73–76 | 78–80 | +3 |
| Varnell | 76–78 | 80–82 | +3–4 |

- Leaders are veteran versions: six Pokémon, fully evolved, newer species that fit their type
  (e.g., Brock: Rhyperior, Tyranitar, Aerodactyl, Gigalith, Kabutops, Golem — **no Geodude**).
- Re-run the simulator on the real Kanto trainer data once it exists; adjust until each boss is 1–3 above.

## 6. Champion defenses (moved after Kanto)

- The title-defense ladder starts **when the player returns from Kanto**, not right after Hall of Fame.
  Story: you come home to defend your title now that everything is done.
- Keep every existing Hoenn challenger (Treston, Wally, Wallace, Steven, rival, Gym Leaders…).
- Add Kanto challengers: the Kanto Gym Leaders, Blue, the Kanto Elite Four.
- **Red** is the final challenger, at the top of the curve (current defenses run 74–90).
- Needs a code change: today the ladder triggers on repeat E4 runs after the Hall of Fame.

## 7. Assets (owner rules)

- **Only FireRed and Emerald tiles and sprites.** No hand-drawn art. Outside assets only if free to use,
  credited, tested in game, and shown to the owner first.
- **Recolours are allowed** as new Varnell-only tileset copies, so no other map changes.
- One map = one tileset pair. Different rooms can use different sets (like Silph Co.).
- Mockups (made from real tiles, renderer not in-game): Varnell Institute public floor (Cinnabar Lab set),
  ARIA core (Aqua Hideout facility set), holding bay with four sealed tanks, recoloured "Void" (calm) and
  "Red Alert" (ARIA firing) — palette swap is a live story trigger, plus a breathing pulse on the tanks.
- Varnell's sprite: an existing scientist/gentleman sprite with a new palette unless a free outside sprite
  is approved.
